# Listing Optimization - maximize free viewership

Every listing gets the full treatment before it goes live:

- **Titles buyers actually search**: front-load brand + the popular/model name people type ("Nike Air Max 90" not "cool sneakers"). Include size, colorway, era, condition keywords. Research what search terms the comps used.
- **Hashtags everywhere they work**: Depop especially (use all available), Grailed tags, keyword-rich Facebook descriptions. Pull from high-traffic tags for that brand/category.
- **Photos optimized to sell**: clean bright background (swap/brighten during processing in [[workflow-process-items]]). If a listing would perform better with a different angle, detail shot, or on-body photo -> ASK Breken to take it, he's game.
- **Fill every field**: right category, brand field, condition, size - complete listings rank higher on every platform.
- **Free bumps**: relist/refresh listings where the platform allows it at no cost, at human pace per [[safety-limits]].
- **Deep comp dive first**: no listing without the research required in [[pricing-rules]].

Breken supplies: brief description + rough (non-firm) price estimate per [[pricing-rules]].

Used by [[workflow-process-items]] and [[workflow-listing]].
