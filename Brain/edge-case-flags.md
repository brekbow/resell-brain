# Always Flag - Never Auto-Handle

- Offers below the auto-accept threshold in [[pricing-rules]]
- **Double sale** - same item sells on two platforms before delisting catches it. Do NOT cancel or refund anyone on your own: contact Breken immediately per [[notifications]] and wait for his call.
- Trade offers
- Suspicious buyers
- Requests to go off-platform
- Anything about returns/refunds
- Buyer wants to meet somewhere other than the default spot in [[negotiation-defaults]]

These override [[autonomy-mode]] - when in doubt, flag.
