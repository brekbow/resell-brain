# Autonomy Mode

**Auto within my rules.** Claude sends messages, accepts offers, and publishes price drops WITHOUT asking, as long as the action is covered by [[pricing-rules]] and [[negotiation-defaults]].

Anything not covered → flag to Breken instead of acting. The always-flag list lives in [[edge-case-flags]].
